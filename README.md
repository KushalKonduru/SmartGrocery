# Smart Pantry

Smart Pantry is a web application designed to help you manage your pantry, create shopping lists, and reduce food waste. It also includes features for a marketplace to sell or donate surplus items.

🌐 **Live Demo:** [https://smart-pantry-management.vercel.app](https://smart-pantry-management.vercel.app)

## Features

*   **Pantry Management:** Keep track of the items in your pantry, including expiration dates.
*   **Shopping List:** Easily create and manage your shopping lists.
*   **Barcode Scanning:** Quickly add items to your pantry or shopping list by scanning their barcodes.
*   **Marketplace:** Sell or donate surplus food items to others in your community.
*   **Recipe Suggestions:** Get recipe suggestions based on the items in your pantry with the help of our Iron Chef assistant.
*   **Achievements:** Earn badges and achievements for managing your pantry effectively and reducing food waste.
